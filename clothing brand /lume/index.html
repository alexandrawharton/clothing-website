Our Story

Fashion with Soul

We believe getting dressed should feel like an act of joy — not an obligation.

💖

Born from a Big Feeling

LUMÉ started in a tiny apartment in Los Angeles when our founder, Sofia, had one burning question: why does getting dressed have to feel so stressful? In 2021, she began designing pieces that felt as good as they looked — clothes that said something, meant something, and brought a little sparkle to ordinary days.

🌍

Sustainably Made, Intentionally Designed

Every garment in the LUMÉ collection is produced in small batches using ethically sourced fabrics — organic cotton, recycled fibers, and low-impact dyes. We partner with certified fair-trade factories and offset 100% of our shipping emissions. Looking cute shouldn't cost the planet.

✨

Inclusive by Design

LUMÉ is made for every body, every background, every era. Our sizing runs XS–4XL, and every style is photographed on a diverse range of models. We design for real life — commutes, coffee dates, dance floors, and everything in between.

🌸

Community First

5% of every purchase goes directly to women-focused nonprofits — funding education, entrepreneurship, and wellness programs around the globe. When you shop LUMÉ, you're not just buying a dress. You're investing in a movement.
